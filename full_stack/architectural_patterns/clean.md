# Introduction

Clean Architecture is an architectural style/pattern proposed by Robert C. Martin in 2012 [1], who also proposed the [Dependency Inversion Principle (DIP)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dependency_inversion_principle) and introduced the [SOLID principles](../design/solid.md). 

It combines the principles of [hexagonal architecture](./hexagonal.md), [onion architecture](./onion.md), and variants.
